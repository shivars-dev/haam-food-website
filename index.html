Our Story

It started on our farm — and with a word from our daughter.

Every season, our family's mango trees give us far more fruit than we could ever eat fresh. Rather than let a single mango go to waste, we started turning them into the same foods our family has always made at home — starting with my mother's mango pickle recipe, which is now 40 years old.

The name Haam comes from our daughter. It's the word she reaches for when she wants to eat — simple, joyful, and completely her own. It felt like exactly the right name for food made with that same instinct: real ingredients, made simply, made to be enjoyed.

— The Haam.food family
