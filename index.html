About Us

It started on our farm — and with a word from our daughter.

Every season, our family farm gives us far more fruit than we could ever eat fresh — mangoes first, with more on the way. Rather than let any of it go to waste, we started turning it into the same foods our family has always made at home, beginning with my mother's mango pickle recipe, now 40 years old.

The name Hamm comes from our daughter. It's the word she reaches for when she wants to eat — simple, joyful, and completely her own. It felt like exactly the right name for food made with that same instinct: real ingredients, made simply, made to be enjoyed.

Hamm.food isn't a mango company — it's a farm-to-food company that happens to have started with mango. As our farm grows into new fruits and vegetables, so will what's on this page.

— The Hamm.food family
